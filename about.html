About

Hi, I'm Nicole.

I'm an artist, writer, and technically minded problem-solver. My work spans SaaS support, web development, graphic design, UX, and healthcare technology.

I like making things and understanding how they work. That has led me to build websites, troubleshoot software, help people learn new tools, and create documentation that's useful when something goes wrong.

I started in healthcare technology, where I helped implement and support a CMMS managing more than 1,600 medical devices. I created SOPs, work instructions, training materials, compliance resources, and end-user documentation for clinical engineering teams.

Later, I moved into web technology and digital publishing. I supported WordPress publishers, troubleshot technical problems, wrote help content and tutorials, and maintained knowledge bases.

I studied graphic design, UX, and front-end development, and mentored students in the Treehouse Front-End Techdegree. I also run Peery Creative, where I create art and maintain my own WordPress and WooCommerce storefront.

I write the docs I always wished I had.
